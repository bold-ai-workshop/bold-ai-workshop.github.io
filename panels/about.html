The premise

Most of modern AI converges to one recipe. Is it the right one?

The field of AI has become synonymous with fast paced research development. While this has been extremely successful in pushing the field forward, it has created a paradox. It has become debatable whether many of the researchers who facilitated AI's biggest leaps would have the freedom to do so in the modern academic ecosystem. The pressure for state of the art has created a culture toward trend-chasing that chokes out the ability of young researchers to pursue ideas that do not align with the main stream.

BOLD AI is the workshop for the alternative architectures, learning rules, and substrates that may be more biologically plausible, more energy efficient, or more mathematically elegant. We aim to foster the next generation of creative and niche research directions that struggle to fit the mold of topical, trendy workshops.

We believe the next paradigm shift in AI architectures will come from platforming researchers who have been thinking beyond what is currently working. Our goal is to give them a space to share their visions.
