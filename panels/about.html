The premise

Most of modern AI converged on one recipe. Is it the right one?

The dominant paradigm — dense transformers trained with backpropagation at ever-larger scale — has been extraordinarily productive. Pushing transformers forward is fruitful for now, but it is just as important to keep exploring more efficient architectures. That exploration, however, has a hard time finding a home amid the research stigma pushing work toward chasing SOTA and bold numbers.

BOLD AI is the workshop for the alternatives: architectures and learning rules drawn from neuroscience, cognitive science, biology, physics, and mathematics. We aim to foster the more creative and niche research directions that struggle to fit the mold of topical, trendy workshops.

We believe the next paradigm shift in AI architectures will come from platforming researchers who have been thinking beyond the current orthodoxy, giving them a space to share their visions.
