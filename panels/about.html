The premise

Most of modern AI converged on one recipe. Intelligence in nature never did.

The dominant paradigm — dense transformers trained with backpropagation at ever-larger scale — has been extraordinarily productive. But it is one point in a vast design space that brains, bodies, and physical systems have been exploring for billions of years.

BOLD AI is a workshop for the alternatives: architectures and learning rules drawn from neuroscience, cognitive science, biology, physics, and mathematics. We bring together researchers working on structured representations, high-dimensional and dynamical computation, learning beyond backprop, and the geometry of intelligence.

Our wager is simple: the principles that let a nervous system compute robustly on noisy hardware with milliwatts of power are principles worth taking seriously as engineering — not just biology.
